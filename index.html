01 · Profile

Calibrating Deep-Time Earth History Across Basins & Biotas

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the State Key Laboratory of Critical Earth Material Cycling and Mineral Deposits, School of Earth Sciences and Engineering, Nanjing University. My current research centers on high-precision Carboniferous and Permian geochronology and integrative stratigraphy—using CA-ID-TIMS zircon U–Pb dating of volcanic ash beds to anchor late Paleozoic timescales, hydrocarbon source rock evolution, and major biotic and paleoclimatic transitions.

Previously, my master's work at the University of Science and Technology of China focused on quantitative paleogeographic reconstructions of the South China Block during the Permian, exploring sea-level dynamics, the Artinskian Warming Event, and paleobiogeographic knowledge graphs. Across both field and laboratory work, I integrate outcrop stratigraphy from Xinjiang, South China, North China, and the Qinghai-Tibetan Plateau with high-resolution radiometric age models.

03 · Research Program

Core Themes & Highlights

High-Precision U–Pb Geochronology

Establishing high-resolution Carboniferous–Permian and Silurian chronostratigraphic frameworks via CA-ID-TIMS zircon U–Pb dating across the Junggar Basin, North China, and South China—constraining source rock deposition, the scissor-like closure of the Paleo-Asian Ocean, and early gnathostome radiation.

GSA Bulletin (2026) · Sci. China Earth Sci. (2026) · EPSL (2026)

Quantitative Paleogeography

Reconstructing dynamic Permian paleogeography and sea-level evolution of the South China and North China blocks, linking lithofacies shifts to the Artinskian Warming Event, coal and hydrocarbon accumulation, and data-driven paleobiogeographic knowledge graphs.

Palaeo3 (2024, 2023, 2020) · J. Earth Sci. (2023)

Integrative Stratigraphy & Biotic Crises

Synthesizing biostratigraphic, geochemical, and radiometric records across China and the Tethyan realm—including the redefinition of the base-Wuchiapingian GSSP/SABS, the end-Guadalupian biotic crisis, and Permian stratigraphy of the Qinghai-Tibetan Plateau.

Nature Comm. (2025) · Episodes (2024) · Geology (2021)
